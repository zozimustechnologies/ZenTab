Zen Tab

A fast, distraction-free new tab page with clock, search, bookmarks, todos, weather, notes, and focus mode — no account required.

Everything you need, nothing you don’t

A complete workspace on every new tab — all data stays in your browser, no sign-in ever required.

🕐

Live Clock & Smart Greeting

See the current time at a glance in 12 h or 24 h format. A personalised greeting adapts to the time of day and can be set to Formal, Semi-formal, or Informal.

🔍

Search + /Shortcut Keys

Type a term or paste a URL to search the web, or define custom /flag shortcuts to jump to any site. Supports a {q} placeholder for passing queries through.

🎯

Favourite Tabs

Pin up to 6 frequently visited sites as icon tiles for one-click access. Each tile shows the site’s favicon. A permanent Add button keeps the 7th slot ready for new pins.

🕐

Recent Sites

Your recent browsing history is surfaced below your favourites with deduplication and filtering, so you can jump back without hunting through tabs or history.

✅

To-Do List

Add, check off, and delete tasks directly on your new tab. Tasks persist across sessions in local storage — no cloud sync required.

📝

Notes

A freeform scratchpad always visible on your new tab — ideal for quick thoughts, URLs to revisit, or reminders you’d otherwise forget.

🌤

Live Weather

Current temperature and conditions for your city, powered by Open-Meteo — fully open-source and privacy-friendly. No API key needed, refreshed on a schedule.

🌟

Focus Mode

Hides non-essential widgets and shows only your tasks and a minimal clock. Designed for deep work sessions where every distraction costs you.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export all settings, shortcuts, tasks, and notes to a single JSON file. Import any previous backup instantly — no account, no cloud, no data left behind.

🔒

Site Blocker Integration

Works alongside the companion Site Blocker extension to hide unwanted domains from your Recent Sites feed. Toggle it without leaving the new tab.

Get started in seconds

Install the extension

Get it from the Edge Add-ons store or the Chrome Web Store.

Open a new tab

Zen Tab replaces your new tab page automatically. Open any new tab and you’ll see it straight away.

Personalise in Settings

Click the gear icon to set your name, clock format, weather city, search shortcuts, and preferred greeting style.

Start being productive

Pin your favourite sites, jot down tasks and notes, and let the weather and clock keep you oriented throughout the day.
